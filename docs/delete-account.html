Delete your Safe Scales account

You can request deletion of your Safe Scales account and associated personal data by emailing our support address. Send the request from the email address registered to your account so we can verify that you own it.

Email an account deletion request

What to include

  • The email address associated with your Safe Scales account.
  • Your username, if you know it.
  • A clear request to delete your account.

Do not include your password. We may contact you if we need additional information to verify the request. After verification, we will delete your account and data linked to it from our active systems, including your profile and saved class, learning-progress, quiz, and in-app customization data. Information retained in backups or where required by law may remain for the applicable retention period.

For other privacy questions, see our privacy policy or email byu.stapl.research@gmail.com.
