Delete your SafeScales account

You can ask us to delete your SafeScales account and its data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email byu.stapl.research@gmail.com with the subject "SafeScales account deletion".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to SafeScales, or include that address in the message.
  3. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account, email address, profile details, lesson progress, quiz answers, and settings are permanently deleted.

What may be kept

De-identified research data that can no longer be linked to you may be kept. Nothing else is retained.
