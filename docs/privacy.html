Safe Scales Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy describes information Safe Scales processes when you use the app and how you can request its deletion.

Information collected

When you create or sign in to an account, Safe Scales processes your email address and password. Account creation also uses the username you provide. Supabase provides the app's account and database services.

When you use the app, it stores information linked to your account to provide its features. This may include class membership, lesson and reading progress, quiz activity, and in-app customizations and items.

How information is used

Account information is used to create and authenticate your account. App data is used to provide learning features and save your progress. Contact information is used to respond to support and account-deletion requests.

Service providers

Supabase processes and stores account and app information on behalf of Safe Scales to provide these services.

Retention and deletion

Account information and associated app data are retained while your account is active. You can request deletion by following the instructions on our account deletion page. Information may be retained where required by law.

Security

We rely on the safeguards provided by the services described above. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's privacy

Safe Scales is an educational app. Parents or guardians who have questions about a child's information or want to request its deletion can contact us using the details below.

Contact
