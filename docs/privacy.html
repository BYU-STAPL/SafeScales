SafeScales Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

SafeScales is a research and education app developed by the BYU STAPL research team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and the basic profile details you provide when you create an account.
  • App activity: your lesson progress, quiz answers, and settings, so the app can save your progress.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment information. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to sign you in, save your progress, and improve the learning content. Data may also be reviewed in de-identified form for BYU research purposes.

Where it is stored

Data is stored securely with our backend provider, Supabase, and is sent over encrypted connections.

Children

If the app is used by students under 13, it should be used with the involvement of a parent, guardian, or teacher.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all of its data at any time. See Delete your account.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
